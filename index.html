Figures come from data.police.uk, which publishes each month's data about seven weeks after the month ends. Areas are North Wales Police neighbourhood policing areas, not council wards.

Locations on the map are approximate: police data places each crime at the centre of a nearby street or area to protect victims' privacy. A crime can be recorded some time after it happened. Small areas can swing a lot from month to month, so look at the trend rather than a single month.
